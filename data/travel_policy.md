# TripWise Travel Policy (Demo)

## General
- TripWise provides guidance for travel planning, baggage, cancellations, and basic travel compliance.
- This is demo data; always confirm with airline/hotel policies.

## Refundable vs Non-refundable
- Refundable fares can typically be canceled for a full refund according to fare rules.
- Non-refundable fares generally do not qualify for a cash refund; a credit may be offered depending on fare rules.
